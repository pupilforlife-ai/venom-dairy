# Dashboard Deferred Features

These Dashboard features are intentionally not completed until their supporting sections provide reliable live data.

## Weekly fuel cost

Depends on the Utilities page recording complete utility periods, costs, and comparison periods. The Dashboard currently shows `—` instead of the old hardcoded `R16,395`.

## Historical yield trend

Depends on dated production and reconciliation history across multiple lots/weeks. The Dashboard currently shows the selected active lot's calculated paneer yield rather than the old mock W20-W24 series.

## Today versus average trend comparisons

Depends on a defined reporting timezone, production date boundaries, and historical comparison data. Current metrics are labeled as current-lot or recorded totals instead of showing mock percentage comparisons.

## Dashboard navigation and drill-down actions

Cards and alerts can later link to the relevant production, cold-chain, packing, utilities, and handover pages once those workflows are fully validated.
